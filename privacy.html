Privacy Policy — Knowledge Human

Last updated: 16 November 2026

“Knowledge Human” is a private tool used only by the owner of the educational channel «إنسان المعرفة» (Knowledge Human) to upload and publish the channel's own videos to the channel's own TikTok account. It also serves the channel's Facebook Pages under the name “Assistant Scheduler”.

What the app does

  • It uploads the channel's own videos, with their captions, to the channel's own TikTok account, on the instruction of the account owner.
  • It reads the basic profile information of that account (display name and avatar) to confirm that it is connected to the right account.
  • It reads the publishing status of the videos it uploaded, to show the owner whether each upload succeeded.

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of TikTok users, viewers or followers.
  • It does not read messages, comments, followers or other users' profiles.
  • It has no public users: only the owner of the channel uses it, for the channel's own account.
  • It shows no advertising and does no tracking.

Where data is kept

  • The access tokens needed to publish are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never sent to any third party.
  • A local log of uploads (date, file name, result) is kept on the same computer for the owner's records.

Data deletion

  • The app holds no personal data of any user, so there is nothing to delete for users.
  • The owner can remove the app's access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions → remove “Knowledge Human”. The local tokens are then deleted from the owner's computer.

Contact

For any question or deletion request: insaanalmaarefa@gmail.com
